# Durable Chat / Research Handoff Protocol

Long sessions are working memory, not a durable record. Before stopping or switching major source passes, commit:

1. Current workflow and stage, exact last fully checked source/claim/batch.
2. The next source/claim/batch to process.
3. Authoritative repository files/IDs and most recent verified bibliography.
4. Unresolved QUESTION and author decisions.
5. Key protocol exceptions, research caveats, and publicity/privacy risks.
6. Which claims remain TO VERIFY and whether any canon was actually integrated.
7. Audit and publication status when relevant.

Prefer a checkpoint in the active dive's scope/research/index rather than multiple competing handoff documents.

For repeated entries, work in bounded batches (often 20–30 compact source claims, less for complex fragments). At a new conversation, read PROJECT-INSTRUCTIONS, this protocol and the active dive index; **resume from the last committed state**, not speculation about prior chat history.

Checkpointing is continuity protection, not a permission gate.

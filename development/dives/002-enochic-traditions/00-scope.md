# Dive 002 — Enochic traditions: historical-source pass

## Binding direction
PROC-004: Research surviving authentic historical and mythological source traditions thoroughly *before* choosing fictional synthesis. DEC-001–007 remain the approved genre/era constraints; no new city technology, supernatural hierarchy, ritual or biological invention should enter canon.

## Main question
What do individual ancient Enochic witnesses actually say about heavenly beings, giant offspring, forbidden teaching, human experiences, judgments and the lineage of related stories? How did their manuscripts and later transmission differ?

## Scope
Pass 2A: 1 Enoch 1–16 and source-critical control, continuing through 17–36; then separate passes for the **Book of Giants**, **Jubilees**, **Genesis Apocryphon**, and related Jewish primeval writings identified by the coverage map.

## Boundaries
- 1 Enoch is a composite, not a single uniformly dated work.
- A modern English translation can confirm a reading in that presentation, not the contents of every ancient fragment.
- Source variations and possibly different literary strata remain **research questions**.
- No Enochic “technology” is automatically canonical, and no source asserts acoustic levitation or genetic engineering.
- Modern conspiracy theories and modern fiction are outside this ancient-source pass.

## Research sources
SRC-012–015 as queued critical editions, SRC-020–026 first Pass 2A read/preview sources, follow-up editions SRC-027 onward.

## Success criteria
Stable claims, actual passage pinpoints, witness matrix, literature/chronology provenance, named limitations, and a precise next source reading. **Do not demand final lore decisions as a research completion condition.**

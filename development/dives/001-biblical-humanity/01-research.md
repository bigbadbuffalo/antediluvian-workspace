## Significant results

- Farming, flocks, city building, mobile pastoral life, music and metals supply a plural human-world starting point; no population, complete institution set or technology tree is given.
- Named lineages are not population censuses. Cain's line has no begetting ages; its chronology cannot be synchronized with Seth's by generation slots.
- Cain's city and Jared's Enoch must remain distinct; the two Lamechs and Methushael/Methuselah likewise.
- Selected Greek and Hebrew readings differ meaningfully. Under explicit continuous-year arithmetic, Hebrew/JPS gives 1656 and selected NETS gives 2242 years at Noah's 600th year. The NETS inputs create a visible Methuselah survival tension.
- Josephus supplies genuine ancient reception about economic/urban control and learned preservation, distinct from Genesis and from archaeological confirmation.
- Uruk/accounting evidence supplies a limited analogy for organizational sophistication; it does not identify Enoch or demonstrate supernatural engineering.
- No new canon, technical mechanism, supernatural identity or chronology selected.

# Antediluvian — Deep Dive Protocol

Adapted from the Umbrathar and Death Dealers workspace workflow, with extra source-critical controls.

## Pipeline

**SCOPE → RESEARCH → WORKSHOP → DECIDE → INTEGRATE → AUDIT → REVIEW → CLOSE**

Stages are substantive work, not ceremonial gates. Proceed automatically when no meaningful author decision is needed. Use development/templates/00-scope.md through 04-audit.md.

## 1. Scope

Record subject, level (cosmic framework / age / society / institution / locality / person), design problem, current canon constraints, linked inbox entries, inspirations, exclusions, dependencies and success criteria. Because this setting depends on testimony from multiple eras, specify which ancient/modern source layers to evaluate. Before finalizing an inspiration-sensitive scope, seek the author's new reference preferences **only if not already given**.

## 2. Research

Document primary passages, witness/edition, language where material, composition vs manuscript date, relevant variants, later reception, scholarship, contrasts, evidence limitations and creative possibilities **separately**. Give each consequential assertion a CLAIM ID. Preserve durable source references in sources/ and research/. Research is non-canon and may challenge the proposed scope.

Do not harmonize contradictions by silently assuming:
- every “son of God” passage refers to the same beings;
- all Enoch literature shares one origin or angelology;
- giants/Watchers/evil spirits are interchangeable;
- Mesopotamian sages or gods are proven to be Nephilim;
- modern occult/conspiracy interpretations preserve an ancient secret tradition.

A comparison can become an intentional **setting synthesis** later, not a fact of the source.

## 3. Workshop

Discuss concepts in clusters, with effects on theology, everyday life, rulers, moral choices, geography, technology and adventures. Use **DECISION**, **IDEA**, **QUESTION**, **REJECT** only as actually applicable. Workshop recency does not override the consolidated decision ledger.

## 4. Decide

Record approved propositions and rationales in 03-decisions.md. Include: what is literally true in the game world; what is merely believed; whether a tradition's details are adopted, modified, rejected, or deliberately mysterious; source IDs; anticipated dependencies. The author decides consequential fictional truth. Late creative changes return to this ledger before further integration.

## 5. Integrate

Change one primary canon article per topic; reference research without copying huge dossiers. Preserve frontmatter status/visibility. Record changed pages, checked dependencies, deferred dependencies, and deliberate status changes. Do not promote unrelated provisional text when editing a page.

## 6. Audit

Check:
1. Continuity and causal coherence.
2. Fit with the project's established theological frame and intended tone.
3. **Source integrity:** exact passages, era/witness, translation, and source-to-claim validity.
4. **Layer separation:** do not confuse ancient attestation, historical verification, later reception, and setting canon.
5. **Modern contamination / derivative copying:** modern RPG or fiction inventions distinguished; avoid close proprietary borrowing.
6. Ordinary-human, ruler-level, and adventure consequences.
7. Generative canon and appropriate development scale.
8. Dependencies, metadata/publication and current **public repository exposure**.

Assign stable audit IDs AF-01 etc when multiple actionable findings exist, with severity and disposition. Mechanical corrections can proceed; lore-altering corrections require authorial judgment.

## 7–8. Review and close

Resolve substantive author questions; record deferments and explicit bypasses. Ensure durable evidence, decisions, canon integration and handoff are saved. Mark the dive CLOSED in DIVE-INDEX. Preserve future questions rather than over-specifying the world.

## Working principle

A good dive produces clearer playable causal pressures with fewer accidental commitments, not the most encyclopedic volume. Ancient parallels should enrich original fiction, not erase the identity of distinct source traditions.

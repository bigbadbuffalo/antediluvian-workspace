# Setting Foundations — Antediluvian

**Approved high-level setting direction (2026-11-10).** These propositions are authoritative for development. This is a **design and era charter**, not an assertion that speculative details are present in ancient source texts. Exact dates, maps, beings, technology and institutions remain subject to research and future author decisions.

## People are the subject (DEC-001)

This is a setting about humans living in the world before the Flood: families, craftspeople, farmers, traders, kings, the dispossessed, communities, religions, laws and wars. Their motives, daily routines, aspirations and choices should drive playable stories. Supernatural beings may rule, terrorize, manipulate, teach or guide them, but do not displace human agency or become the entire subject.

In every major supernatural development, ask: *What does this mean for ordinary people, for human rulers and for adventures?*

## A mature age of the Nephilim (DEC-002)

Play is centered on a developed antediluvian age in which the Nephilim are already an established presence and human societies have had time to grow. The exact date, lifespans, degrees of corruption, relations between communities and the nature of the Nephilim are **not** fixed.

## Before the Flood, not "the Flood RPG" (DEC-003)

The Flood is a known element of the larger biblical narrative, and the reader may perceive shadows of eventual judgment. It is not the standard campaign arc, plot clock or victory/failure condition. The era is a real, lived-in world for its characters, not merely a prologue to its destruction.

## Genre and experience (DEC-004)

Combine **biblical dark fantasy** with **mythic sword-and-sorcery**. Prioritize evocative human adventures, rival powers, strange places, worldly danger, supernatural pressure, and consequential moral choices. The setting should be playable without every scenario revolving around angelology or prophecy.

## Mythic source integration (DEC-005)

Genesis is the central narrative framework. Study ancient Jewish extrabiblical material and the ancient Near East on their own terms, then potentially widen to other *historically attested* ancient or prehistoric traditions. Fictional synthesis may intentionally combine sources, but must not be passed off as a demonstrated historical connection. Speculative modern theories may inspire clearly identified fictional additions.

## Great cities and extraordinary sophistication (DEC-006)

Cain-associated cities and their civilizations are a major worldbuilding opportunity. They should be explored as **unusually sophisticated, powerful and perhaps extraordinary urban cultures**, potentially aided or distorted by knowledge from supernatural sources. Their technological ceiling, social effects and mechanisms have not yet been selected.

**Important source distinction:** Genesis 4:17 mentions Cain building a city; a network of technologically advanced Cainite metropolises is a *setting-development expansion*, not something that verse explicitly establishes. Even where the fictional world adopts such cities, do not label their scale, devices or social order as archaeologically verified. Keep extraordinary capabilities constrained by costs, institutions, dependencies and consequences rather than granting arbitrary modern technologies.

## An alternative path to greatness (DEC-007)

Antediluvian sophistication should not resemble a typical technological progression into modern industrial civilization. **Do not default to firearms, jet planes, modern factories or today's machinery** as the signs that a great city is advanced. Its astonishing abilities may follow radically different paths: **sorcery, ritual or esoteric knowledge, engineered resonance and sound, proposed acoustic levitation, monumental architecture, materials and astronomical understanding, and interventions by supernatural powers**.

These examples are **promising directions, not a fixed inventory of working machines or magic systems**. Popular lost-civilization and alternative-archaeology discussions (including ones encountered through Joe Rogan's podcast) express the desired imaginative direction but are **not primary ancient testimony or independent historical evidence**. In particular, claims that ancient people levitated megaliths with sound require their own evidentiary audit; their use in this fantasy world would be an explicit invention or synthesis until otherwise documented.

When designing any extraordinary capability, determine who knows it, who controls it, what it requires or costs, and what it means for the people laboring, trading, worshiping and living under such powers. Different polities may advance in different domains.

## Still undecided

- Exact date and geography.
- Which specific Watcher, giant and demon traditions become true in-world.
- Which specific alternative technologies or supernatural arts function, how they work, and how advanced particular Cain-associated cities are relative to rival cultures.
- Detailed cultures, institutions and social orders.
- Specific syntheses of Mesopotamian or wider mythic traditions.
- Rules system and campaign-scale conventions.

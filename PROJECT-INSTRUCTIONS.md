# Antediluvian — Project Instructions

**Status:** Governing workspace instructions. Read before substantial research or setting-development work.

## Mission

Develop an original tabletop RPG world set before the biblical Flood. The **narrative/theological foundation** is Genesis 1–11. Investigate the Enochic Watchers tradition and other ancient Jewish and Near Eastern traditions with historical care; use later Christian interpretations, modern speculation, and modern fiction only with explicit source labels. Preserve artistic freedom without mislabeling inventions as ancient teachings.

This is a creative reconstruction, **not an archaeological assertion** that supernatural events have been independently verified. Distinguish what texts say, what interpreters conclude, what historians can establish about the texts, and what the author chooses to make fictional truth.

## Repository authority and continuity

The repository is the durable authoring workspace. Chat and research sessions are working contexts, not authoritative long-term state. Before editing, read pertinent repository files, not just remembered discussion. Preserve consequential decisions, citations, scope changes, contradictions, unresolved questions, and handoffs in the repository. **Tools produce work; the repository preserves state.**

Guidance order: (1) explicit latest author direction, (2) this file, (3) applicable specialized protocols, (4) task-specific records/templates. Escalate genuine conflicting source/canon choices; do not create ceremonial approval gates.

## Hard separations

- **Sources:** evidence of what a particular text/tradition states, its date, authorship, witness history, and transmission; not automatically historical fact or setting canon.
- **Research:** documented evidence, interpretation, competing readings, scholarly disagreement, and hypothetical applications. Non-canon.
- **Development:** IDEA, QUESTION, REJECT, and approved DECISION material, with rationale.
- **Canon:** explicitly approved propositions about the fictional world; one primary home per topic.
- **In-world claims:** religions, rumors, propaganda, misunderstandings and contested traditions may be canonical *as beliefs* without being objectively true.

A source's religious canonical status is context-specific; do not treat all Christians, Jews, or ancient readers as sharing a uniform canon. Distinguish composition date, manuscript date, and modern publication date. Absence of ancient attestation is not proof of impossibility, but unsupported modern claims cannot be presented as ancient evidence.

## Source hierarchy for research (not a canon vote)

1. Biblical primary passages (especially Genesis 1–11), in their textual traditions.
2. Ancient extrabiblical Jewish and related texts (e.g., 1 Enoch, Book of Giants, Jubilees, Genesis Apocryphon).
3. Ancient Near Eastern parallels studied independently (e.g., Atrahasis, Gilgamesh, Sumerian King List, apkallu traditions).
4. Ancient/medieval/early-modern exegesis and reception.
5. Modern religious speculation, esotericism, conspiracy claims and alternative history.
6. Modern fantasy, RPGs and entertainment; **quarantined as fiction**, not evidence of antiquity.

Research chronology and influence, do not assume direct borrowing or identification merely from resemblance. Modern claims about giant skeletons, suppressed texts, advanced lost technology, or secret bloodlines need the same source audit as other claims. Avoid circulating invented quotations or undocumented facts.

## Project's provisional creative stance

- **Biblical core; ancient expansion; independent creative synthesis.**
- God is approached as real, sovereign, just, and good **within the proposed fictional worldview**; interpretive questions and details remain open.
- No settled campaign start date, geographic reconstruction, technology level, rules system, or final treatment of Watchers/Nephilim exists yet.
- The Flood is part of the overarching narrative framework; where play is set relative to it remains undecided.
- Ordinary human society, cultural systems and daily life matter as much as exceptional supernatural beings.
- Avoid importing World of Darkness, Vampire: The Masquerade, or other modern creative IP as alleged historical tradition.

These are **project direction**, not an authorized encyclopedic canon. Do not fill unspecified gaps with invented binding lore.

## Development workflow

Formal dives follow [development/DEEP-DIVE-PROTOCOL.md](development/DEEP-DIVE-PROTOCOL.md):
**SCOPE → RESEARCH → WORKSHOP → DECIDE → INTEGRATE → AUDIT → REVIEW → CLOSE**.

Mark outcomes **DECISION / IDEA / QUESTION / REJECT**. Only approved DECISION material enters canon. Research findings and workshop variants never silently become facts. A consolidated decisions file outranks older workshop discussion. If a new consequential choice arises during integration/audit, return it to DECIDE first.

Review relevant [development/IDEA-INBOX.md](development/IDEA-INBOX.md) before a new dive. If important inspirations or analogues were not already provided, invite author input before finalizing an inspiration-sensitive scope; otherwise proceed.

## Research execution

- Start from the exact primary passage, document/fragment ID or source edition whenever possible.
- Preserve **exact pinpoint reference** and usable bibliography, plus verified URL/edition when available.
- Mark direct text, translation choice, textual variant, commentator interpretation, modern reconstruction, and creative speculation separately.
- State uncertainty, competing readings, damaged fragments and scholarly minority positions.
- Identify modern fictional contamination **without making blanket assumptions** that every shared motif was invented by fiction.
- Do not fabricate scholarship, verified dates, scholarly consensus, quotations, sources or alleged hidden evidence.
- Durable source records should use [sources/SOURCE-ENTRY-TEMPLATE.md](sources/SOURCE-ENTRY-TEMPLATE.md); consequential claims use [research/SOURCE-AND-CLAIM-REGISTER.md](research/SOURCE-AND-CLAIM-REGISTER.md).
- Do not paste entire copyrighted translations or scans. Store permissible brief quotations, summaries, and bibliographic pointers; respect licensing.
- For long research passes, follow [development/CHAT-ROLLOVER-PROTOCOL.md](development/CHAT-ROLLOVER-PROTOCOL.md).

## Scale and generative canon

**Cosmic/primeval framework → world/age → civilization/polity → institution/community → locality/person.** Adapt scale to the question; do not force all material into predetermined categories. Establish enough causal pressures to let a GM create additional compatible places and adventures, but do not exhaustively specify every detail. Audit effects on ordinary lives, powerful actors, and playable conflicts.

**Relations to test.** Jubilees 5:6–10 and Watchers 10 have more specific overlap in the order of fathers watching offspring die and confinement than a generic shared judgment theme. The common material supports literary comparison without settling how it traveled. The shared three-part terminology of Syncellus 7:2 and Jubilees 7:22 occurs in different presentations and sequences. Compare the relative antiquity of their actual witnesses. Genesis Apocryphon and Jubilees share Batenosh/Betanosh and a separate complex of territorial division (1Q20 XVI–XVII, Jubilees 8:11–9:15). The evidence for dependence or a common source can differ for the two complexes. In Giants, Gilgamesh provides a positive anchor for cross-cultural tradition while individual epic motifs and borrowing routes remain debated.

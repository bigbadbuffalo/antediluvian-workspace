# Pass 3 — Atomic claims (watchers)

Checked 2026-11-10. These entries inherit dates, manuscript conditions and interpretation controls from the [associated dossier](BOOK-OF-WATCHERS.md) and edition details from the [source registers](../../sources/SOURCE-REGISTER.md). Book of the Watchers was composed in Second Temple Aramaic, with constituent dates debated. Dates of the actual Aramaic, Greek and Geʿez witnesses are in the linked dossier. OCP's comparison uses edited material and the eighteenth-century Rylands p for its continuous Geʿez text. Confidence refers to correct attribution and accessible evidence. No proposed setting use is adopted.

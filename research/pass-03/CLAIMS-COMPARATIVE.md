## CLAIM-102 — The Book of Giants names Gilgamesh

- **Source and pinpoint.** SRC-037, SRC-040, SRC-046. 4Q531 22d:12 (older 17:12); 4Q530 2 ii:2 per Stuckenbruck.
- **Evidence and reading.** TEXT. Marro prints variant spellings with damage/joins. The passages do not settle his role in the fate of the giants.
- **Verification.** VERIFIED identification in editions; 4Q530 limits partial.
- **Composition and witness.** Hellenistic Aramaic work; Qumran witnesses.
- **Setting.** UNDECIDED.

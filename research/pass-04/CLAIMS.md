## CLAIM-126 — Milik supplies the 3000-cubit wording in both early copies

- **Source and pinpoint.** SRC-053, SRC-054. Milik 4QEnᵃ 1 iii.16, pp. 150–151, 341, and 4QEnᵇ 1 ii.20–22, pp. 166–168, 344. DEAPS 4Q201 and 4Q202 reading panes.
- **Evidence and reading.** VARIANT. Milik prints the entire measurement within his restoration in both places. These two restored readings cannot serve as two Aramaic attestations of the number. Milik also cautions about space and fragment placement in 4Q202. His separate comparison to cedar height in Damascus Document II.18, pp. 57–58, uses 30 cubits as a hypothetical reduction.
- **Verification.** VERIFIED bracket boundaries through page transcription and digital edition. Printed plates and small-piece alignment pending.
- **Setting.** UNDECIDED.

## CLAIM-129 — Syncellus gives the tenth rank to Azalzel and Azael in different clauses

- **Source and pinpoint.** SRC-024, SRC-052, SRC-055. Syncellus 6:7 and 8:1 in the Black transcription. Compare Akhmim 6:7 and 8:1 and OCP Rylands 8:1.

- **Verification.** VERIFIED in named Greek presentations. Earliest form and relationship to other Azazel traditions UNRESOLVED.
- **Setting.** UNDECIDED.

## CLAIM-133 — Michael's marginal Enoch quotation assigns Kokabel the fourth rank

- **Source and pinpoint.** SRC-023, SRC-034, SRC-055, SRC-057. Chronicle I.4 margin, Chabot French I pp. 7–8 and Syriac IV pp. 3–4 as reproduced in Bhayro p. 356. Drawnel §2.7, f. 5r–v margins.
- **Evidence and reading.** TEXT. Chabot prints twenty leaders with a note citing the Greek for the number. The continuation puts Kokabel fourth and attributes astronomy to him. Chabot's translation specifies solar movements and 360 signs. Syncellus 6:7 puts Chobabiel fourth. The astronomical 360 carries no information about giant stature.
- **Verification.** VERIFIED quotation in the edition and selected Syriac hand-copy. Direct manuscript collation pending.
- **Setting.** UNDECIDED.

## CLAIM-134 — Michael's Cain and Seth identifications belong to the Chronicle narrative

- **Verification.** VERIFIED selected Chronicle translation and Bhayro's account. Earliest source of the reception tradition UNRESOLVED.
- **Setting.** UNDECIDED.

# Antediluvian — Historical and Mythological Source Coverage Map

**Policy (PROC-004):** Cover actual surviving and historically documented traditions before authorial synthesis. This is a **living research bibliography**, not proof that every named source concerns the antediluvian era or that all communities shared one mythology.

## Definitions

- **DISCOVERED:** Identified in credible bibliography, but no passage-level reading completed.
- **READ (bounded):** Relevant passages checked in a named accessible edition/translation; critical apparatus may remain incomplete.
- **WITNESS CHECKED:** Specific manuscript/fragment or scholarly edition checked, with text, date, reconstruction boundaries recorded.
- **RECEPTION MAPPED:** Later historically attested interpretations assigned to identifiable dates, communities and texts.
- **LIMITED:** Inaccessible, lost, untranslated, too damaged or uncertain; evidence limits documented.
- **BROADLY SURVEYED:** All *reasonably accessible and materially relevant* primary families checked, significant variants and contrary traditions recorded, outstanding gaps explicitly named. This is a practical milestone, **not literal omniscience**.

## Broad-source completion standard

A future claim that “we have surveyed the ancient relevant sources” must include (a) a searchable bibliography of all major families, (b) what exact passages/versions were read, (c) which witness dates and variants were checked, (d) where scholarship disagrees, (e) which books or languages were inaccessible, and (f) a documented reasoning for leaving marginal/unrelated traditions outside the current scope. **Never say every ancient myth has been exhausted.**

Do **not** open a collective creative DECIDE round or select fictional harmonizations until the author sees this coverage status and chooses to move from discovery to synthesis. No need for recurrent approval to continue ordinary source reading.

### Candidate first deep dives

- 001 — Genesis and early humanity: Cain, Seth, cities, family lines, crafts and the limits of textual testimony.
- 002 — Human societies in the mature Nephilim age: cities, economies, labor, institutions, inequality and play.
- 003 — Watchers and Nephilim as social, religious and political forces; divergent Enochic traditions.
- 004 — Cain-associated great cities: plausible engineering, extraordinary technology, costs, and provenance audit of modern claims.
- 005 — Mesopotamian primeval mythologies, comparative cosmology and later wider source traditions.

**The Flood is contextual:** research it enough to understand the traditions and foreshadowing, not as the campaign's central subject. Apply a **people-first test** to every supernatural deep dive: what changes for a farmer, artisan, laborer, trader, soldier, parent, ruler or worshiper?

These are **suggested subjects**, not opened dives or authorized canon decisions. Ask for additional inspiration only where not already provided.

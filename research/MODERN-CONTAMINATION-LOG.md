## Topics to investigate first

- Conflation of fallen Watchers with every tradition about Satan or demons.
- Turning Sumerian apkallu into named biblical Watchers without clear argument.
- Transplanting later giant peoples directly into Genesis 6.
- Atlantis, secret antediluvian technologies and ancient astronaut retellings.
- Alleged giant-skeleton coverups and suppressed biblical documents.
- Cain/Lilith/Nephilim mythologies in modern games, notably Vampire: The Masquerade and World of Darkness.

This list flags **research questions**, not assertions that all these ideas have zero ancient antecedents. Document actual textual continuity, later transformations and genuinely novel elements.

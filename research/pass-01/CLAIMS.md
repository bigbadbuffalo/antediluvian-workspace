# Pass 1 — Atomic claims

**Checked 2026-11-10. Research only.** The [master register](../SOURCE-AND-CLAIM-REGISTER.md) indexes these entries. [Source profiles](../../sources/FOUNDATIONAL-CATALOGUE.md) supply edition, composition versus surviving-witness history, rights and access limitations.

## Inherited fields and verification standard

Each entry below explicitly inherits its cited SRC profile's dates and witness limitations; that is part of the claim record, not an assertion that an apparatus was collated. Unless stated otherwise: **setting canon UNDECIDED; confidence HIGH in attribution to the inspected translation; last checked 2026-11-10; potential RPG use IDEA only**, developed in the [workshop](../../development/dives/001-biblical-humanity/02-workshop.md). VERIFIED is limited to that attribution; it does not mean independently established primeval history. PARTIAL or UNRESOLVED always names the missing step. A negative statement is bounded to the cited passage or corpus, never proof that something could not exist.

### CLAIM-001 — The divine image is attributed to male and female humanity

- **Proposition/source/layer:** Genesis 1:26–28 describes humanity, male and female, in God's image; SRC-001, Hebrew/JPS; TEXT.
- **Explicit content:** Image, blessing, multiplication and dominion are assigned to humanity in the passage. No Nephilim or particular city holds an exclusive patent on this status.
- **Interpretive steps/contrasts:** Political or theological theories of the image require further argument. A fictional ruler might deny another person's dignity, but that ruler's propaganda must not silently replace the textual attribution.
- **Verification/dates:** VERIFIED text; inherit SRC-001. **Use:** Human moral agency and stakes across social ranks; no particular legal constitution follows.

### CLAIM-002 — Human work precedes the expulsion

- **Proposition/source/layer:** The human is placed in the garden to tend/keep it before the disobedience narrative; Genesis 2:15, SRC-001; TEXT.
- **Explicit content:** A task involving the garden is already present.
- **Interpretive steps/contrasts:** “All labor is a curse” overstates the narrative: painful toil appears later (CLAIM-004). A complete theology of priestly service cannot be established from this verse alone.
- **Verification/dates:** VERIFIED; SRC-001. **Use:** Skilled work can have dignity even in a morally dangerous civilization.

### CLAIM-003 — Genesis articulates a household bond

- **Proposition/source/layer:** Genesis 2:24 describes leaving father and mother, cleaving to one's wife and becoming one flesh; SRC-001; TEXT.

- **Verification/dates:** VERIFIED; SRC-001. **Use:** Family loyalty and competing household obligations; detailed institutions remain invented or analogical.

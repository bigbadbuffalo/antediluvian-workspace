## 3. Derived chronology and visible contradiction

Assumptions: (1) start Adam at relative year 0; (2) add each named begetting age consecutively; (3) treat figures as continuous ordinary years with no omitted intervals; (4) add 600 at Noah's birth using 7:6. These are explicit calculation conventions, not a textual endorsement of every chronological interpretation. An epoch using year 1 would require consistent relabelling. No BCE conversion is warranted without an independently selected anchor.

| Event | Hebrew/JPS relative year | Selected NETS relative year | Calculation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Adam | 0 | 0 | Chosen arithmetic origin |
| Seth born | 130 | 230 | Adam age |
| Enosh born | 235 | 435 | Previous birth + Seth age |
| Kenan born | 325 | 625 | + Enosh age |
| Mahalalel born | 395 | 795 | + Kenan age |
| Jared born | 460 | 960 | + Mahalalel age |
| Enoch born | 622 | 1122 | + Jared age |
| Methuselah born | 687 | 1287 | + Enoch age |
| Lamech born | 874 | 1454 | + Methuselah age |
| Noah born | **1056** | **1642** | + Lamech age |
| Enoch taken | 987 | 1487 | Enoch birth + 365 |
| Lamech dies | 1651 | 2207 | Lamech birth + stated total |
| Methuselah dies | **1656** | **2256** | Methuselah birth + 969 |
| Flood at Noah 600 | **1656** | **2242** | Noah birth + 600 |

Reproduce the two main sums directly:

`130 + 105 + 90 + 70 + 65 + 162 + 65 + 187 + 182 + 600 = 1656`

`230 + 205 + 190 + 170 + 165 + 162 + 165 + 167 + 188 + 600 = 2242`

**Tension preserved:** In the selected NETS figures, Methuselah's calculated death is 14 years after the Flood begins. This conflicts with straightforwardly combining those figures with Genesis 7:21–23's destruction account and 7:13's listed human Ark occupants, which do not include him. This table does not invent a hidden voyage, change his age to another number or select a superior manuscript. The Hebrew arithmetic puts his death in the Flood year but does not establish the exact day or cause of death. These are CLAIM-030's explicitly derived results, not separately narrated death scenes.

For the project, the table establishes genuine chronological choices and the limits of easy harmonization. It does not make the campaign a countdown. Before a date is selected, resolve how common long lives are, whether the chronology is continuous in-world and what mature Nephilim presence actually requires.

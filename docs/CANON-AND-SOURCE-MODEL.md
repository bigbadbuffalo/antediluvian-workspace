# Canon, Evidence and Source Model

This project requires **two independent axes**: (A) what a source says or what historical scholarship supports; (B) what becomes true in the RPG world. Neither axis can substitute for the other.

## A. Historical/source classification

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| TEXT | Explicitly attested in a specified primary passage/witness. |
| VARIANT | Textually different or damaged witness/translation matters. |
| RECEPTION | Attested later interpretive teaching (identify community and period). |
| SCHOLARLY | Modern academic argument or reconstruction (cite and characterize disagreement). |
| SPECULATION | Unverified, conjectural or modern alternative-history claim. |
| FICTION | Modern authored fictional addition or IP; never historical testimony. |

Use exact passage or manuscript identifiers, estimated date of composition vs surviving witness, original language where relevant, edition/translation, and verification status. “Attested ancient tradition” does not mean the narrated supernatural event has been historically established. “Biblical” does not mean all biblical traditions interpret the passage alike.

## B. Fictional setting decisions

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| canon | Approved and established setting truth (or explicitly established in-world belief). |
| provisional | Preferred draft subject to revision. |
| disputed | Author has not decided among competing accounts. |
| idea | Candidate, not adopted. |
| superseded | Retained for traceability, not current authority. |

**DECISION** is an author-approved proposition recorded in a dive's consolidated ledger or [development/DECISION-LOG.md](../development/DECISION-LOG.md). A source record or detailed research dossier cannot canonize itself.

## Authority precedence for the setting

1. Explicit author direction, including newly approved revisions.
2. Approved, most recent, applicable DECISION ledgers.
3. Existing approved canon articles.
4. Provisional setting notes.
5. Research, external texts, workshop drafts and idea inbox — non-governing until adopted.

An in-world rumor may be canon as a rumor; this does not establish its content as fact. If actual continuity contradictions arise, log them in [development/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md](../development/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md). Do not reconcile silently.

## Source-claim-example (method illustration only)

**Claim:** “The disembodied spirits of dead giants act as evil spirits.”  
**Attestation:** 1 Enoch 15:8–12 in the Book of the Watchers (verify wording in a cited edition).  
**Layer:** TEXT for the Enochic tradition; not a literal assertion from Genesis.  
**Setting canon:** UNDECIDED.  
**Next action:** Verify edition/passage, record potential textual questions and alternative views before proposing canon.

## Suggested lore article frontmatter

When substantive canon begins, use a Markdown frontmatter template at [canon/ARTICLE-TEMPLATE.md](../canon/ARTICLE-TEMPLATE.md). Source references are links to *research*, not indicators that the source necessarily wins the canon decision.

## Publication

Publication visibility (player/gm/spoiler) is distinct from both historical classification and canon status. Future exporters must fail closed on unmarked pages; **GitHub repository visibility is a separate security setting**.

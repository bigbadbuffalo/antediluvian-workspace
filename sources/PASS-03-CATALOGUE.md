# Pass 3 — Annotated Enochic source catalogue

Updated 2026-11-10. The [main register](SOURCE-REGISTER.md) indexes these additions. The [foundational catalogue](FOUNDATIONAL-CATALOGUE.md) retains existing records with Pass 3 access notes. Ancient works are attributed to Enoch, Moses, or other figures as part of their literary framing, where applicable. Edition headings identify modern scholarship. Each source's relevance is documentary. The complete 1 Enoch and Jubilees have scriptural standing in Ethiopian Orthodox tradition. The source profiles record access and pinpoints for independent review. Modern editions and translations retain their publishers' rights except where openly licensed or public domain. Only short readings, references and paraphrases are recorded.

## SRC-034 — Drawnel's Enoch witness bibliography

- **Edition and access.** Henryk Drawnel, “An Introductory Bibliography for the Study of 1 Enoch,” The Biblical Annals 9 (2019), 101–131. [Source](https://bibliotekanauki.pl/articles/1051067.pdf).
- **Evidence and verification.** SCHOLARLY. Selected witness tables and version descriptions checked. Checked 2026-11-10.
- **Ancient composition and witness.** Second Temple Aramaic witnesses through medieval/early modern transmission.
- **Passages and limits.** Table 1 pp. 102–103; Akhmim and Syncellus §2.3; Coptic, Latin and Syriac §§2.5–2.7. Chapter listings record partial overlaps. Distinguish this publication from the full 2019 Cave 4 edition.

## SRC-036 — John Rylands Ethiopic manuscript 23

- **Edition and access.** University of Manchester Library, Ethiopic MS 23, Enoch, Job, and the Book of Kings. Catalogue revised from Stefan Strelcyn by Jeremy Penner and Jeremy Brown. [Source](https://www.digitalcollections.manchester.ac.uk/view/MS-ETHIOPIC-00023/3).
- **Evidence and verification.** MANUSCRIPT. Holding and date checked; text via SRC-024. Checked 2026-11-10.
- **Ancient composition and witness.** Eighteenth-century Geʿez codex of earlier translations; 1 Enoch originally a composite.
- **Passages and limits.** This codex is the basis of the Geʿez pane in OCP, which also exposes Bertalotto corrections. Catalogue control does not establish priority across Ethiopic recensions. Compare Knibb 1978 for multi-witness apparatus.

## SRC-037 — Marro's critical edition of Book of Giants fragments

- **Edition and access.** Iñaki Marro Sánchez, The Book of Giants: A Critical Edition of the Aramaic Fragments in their Ancient Scribal and Tradition-Historical Context (LMU Munich dissertation, 2025), DOI 10.5282/edoc.36601. [Source](https://edoc.ub.uni-muenchen.de/36601/).
- **Evidence and verification.** PRIMARY EDITION / SCHOLARLY. Chapter 5 inventory and selected transcriptions, translations and comparisons checked. Checked 2026-11-10.
- **Ancient composition and witness.** Hellenistic Second Temple composition; separately dated Qumran Aramaic copies and later Manichaean Turfan witnesses.
- **Passages and limits.** Ch. 3 Watchers comparisons, ch. 4 characters and humans, ch. 5 fragment apparatus and ch. 6 Manichaean edition/sources. Record readings as edition-dependent. Recheck individual joins in Puech and Stuckenbruck.

## SRC-038 — Morano's Manichaean Giants survey

- **Evidence and verification.** SCHOLARLY / PRIMARY EDITION. Published fragment list and M 813/I edition discussion checked selectively. Checked 2026-11-10.

- **Passages and limits.** Control text versus excerpt/quotation and works on the same manuscript. ## SRC-039 — Henning's Manichaean Book of Giants edition

- **Edition and access.** W. B. Henning, “The Book of the Giants,” BSOAS 11 (1943), 52–74. [Source](https://sacred-texts.com/chr/giants/giants.htm).
- **Evidence and verification.** PRIMARY EDITION. Selected apparatus and translation, with Marro Appendix I. Checked 2026-11-10.

## SRC-040 — Stuckenbruck's Giants sequencing study

- **Edition and access.** Loren T. Stuckenbruck, “The Sequencing of Fragments Belonging to the Qumran Book of Giants,” JSP 16 (1997), 3–24. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1177/095182079700001601).
- **Evidence and verification.** SCHOLARLY. Selected accessible notes on named figures and narrative order checked. Checked 2026-11-10.
- **Ancient composition and witness.** Aramaic Qumran fragments and Manichaean parallels, different compositions and witnesses.
- **Passages and limits.** Tests ordering including 2Q26 and 6Q8 2 against M 101 j. Bibliographic follow-up: his 2016 reconstruction of 4Q530, pp. 129–142 in Ancient Tales of Giants from Qumran and Turfan. This article is distinct from the 1997 critical monograph SRC-013.

## SRC-041 — Machiela's diplomatic Genesis Apocryphon dissertation

- **Edition and access.** Daniel A. Machiela, The Genesis Apocryphon (1Q20): A Reevaluation of Its Text, Interpretive Character, and Relationship to the Book of Jubilees (Notre Dame dissertation, 2007). [Source](https://orion-bibliography.huji.ac.il/node/55384).
- **Evidence and verification.** PRIMARY EDITION / SCHOLARLY. II–V text and selected commentary sections checked. Checked 2026-11-10.
- **Ancient composition and witness.** Aramaic Hellenistic-era composition, 1Q20 late first c. BCE/early first c. CE palaeographic center.
- **Passages and limits.** Translation pp. 101–106, composition and Noachic comparanda pp. 27–47, manuscript date pp. 298–301. Read via [searchable thesis copy](https://doczz.net/doc/5270452/the-genesis-apocryphon--1q20-); Orion controls attribution. The revised 2009 Brill edition and physical witness comparison remain follow-ups.

## SRC-042 — VanderKam's 2018 Jubilees translation

- **Edition and access.** James C. VanderKam, translation prepared for the 2018 Hermeneia commentary, with digital enhancements by the Jubilees Palimpsest Project at St. Mary’s University. [Source](https://palimpsest.stmarytx.edu/thanneken/2018/VanderKam%282018%29Jubilees-Translation.html).
- **Evidence and verification.** PRIMARY TRANSLATION. Jubilees 3–10 and contextual 1 read. Checked 2026-11-10.
- **Ancient composition and witness.** Hebrew composition mid-second c. BCE estimate, early Hebrew fragments and medieval Geʿez full copies.
- **Passages and limits.** Check digital 1989 differences and underlined Scrolls evidence where marked. 4:15 translation and 7:22, 8:3 and 10:1–14 are priorities. This source is the published translation, not the full Hermeneia commentary/apparatus.

## SRC-043 — Qimron's Hebrew Jubilees fragments

- **Edition and access.** Elisha Qimron, The Dead Sea Scrolls: The Hebrew Writings, vol. 2 (2013), reissued in The Qumran Texts: Composite Edition (2020), DOI 10.5281/zenodo.3737950. [Source](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3737950).
- **Evidence and verification.** PRIMARY EDITION. 11Q12 printed pp. 228–229 visually inspected. Checked 2026-11-10.
- **Ancient composition and witness.** Hebrew Jubilees mid-second c. BCE; 11Q12 first c. CE.
- **Passages and limits.** 11Q12 frgs. 1–7, especially 4:6–11 and 5:1–2. Pale and bracketed matter is editorially supplied. Frgs. 2–3 bracket 4:15 without giving its mission clause. Edition CC BY 4.0; no facsimile copied.

## SRC-044 — Abate's study of the dead in Watchers 22

- **Edition and access.** Adam Michael Abate, The Domains and Procedures of the Dead in the Book of the Watchers: A Liturgy from Hell (Ohio State University MA thesis, 2024). [Source](https://etd.ohiolink.edu/acprod/odb_etd/ws/send_file/send?accession=osu1721150632906397&disposition=inline).
- **Evidence and verification.** PRIMARY TEXT PRESENTATION / SCHOLARLY. Greek and Aramaic appendices and selected sections of chapters 2–3 checked. Checked 2026-11-10.
- **Ancient composition and witness.** Ancient Enochic chapter preserved in fragmentary Aramaic, Greek Akhmim and later Geʿez.
- **Passages and limits.** Appendix pp. 55–56 reproduces Drawnel's 4Q206 frg. 2 ii and 4Q205 frgs. 1–2 with restoration marks and Abate's translation. Treatment of the western mountain, legal appeal, underworld geography and proposed Mesopotamian/Ugaritic and Greek parallels. Inference attributed to author, not part of the ancient text.

## SRC-045 — Hanneken on Latin Jubilees

- **Edition and access.** Todd R. Hanneken, “The Book of Jubilees in Latin,” in The Textual History of the Bible, vol. 2 (Brill, 2019), §8.5; author's pre-publisher copy. [Source](https://jubilees.stmarytx.edu/Hanneken_2019_BookJubileesLatin.html).
- **Evidence and verification.** SCHOLARLY. Transmission and manuscript coverage checked. Checked 2026-11-10.
- **Ancient composition and witness.** Fifth-century Latin witness to ancient Hebrew composition via Greek; Geʿez copies medieval.
- **Passages and limits.** Biblioteca Ambrosiana C 73 inf preserves portions of chs. 13–49. Online imaging and Ceriani 1861 edition are linked in the study. Limited Greek citations and Syriac paraphrases have different evidentiary force.

## SRC-051 — Jacobus on Akhmim and Qumran scribal practice

- **Edition and access.** Helen R. Jacobus, “The influence of ancient Jewish scribal practices on Codex Panopolitanus (the Akhmim Fragment): Links between the Book of the Watchers in 1 Enoch, the Gospel of Peter and the Apocalypse of Peter,” *Journal for the Study of the Pseudepigrapha* (2026), DOI 10.1177/09518207261422280. [Publisher text](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/09518207261422280).
- **Evidence and verification.** SCHOLARLY / PRIMARY EDITION PRESENTATION. Akhmim and Qumran comparison, printed transcriptions and figure captions checked 2026-11-10. The original photograph hosts did not load during the check.
- **Ancient composition and witness.** Second Temple Watchers text. Late Second Temple Aramaic 4Q206 and late antique Greek Cairo 10759. Modern transcriptions and translations are a separate layer.
- **Passages and limits.** 4Q206 frg. 1 ll. 1–2 and Akhmim's change of hand at 14:22; 4Q206 frg. 5 at 32:6b after the Greek stops. Jacobus's theory of intentionally arranged extracts remains an interpretation. Her report of the angel's name at 32:6 in Ethiopic differs from the OCP Rylands English reading. Compare the precise Ethiopic editions before assigning the difference to a scribal variant.

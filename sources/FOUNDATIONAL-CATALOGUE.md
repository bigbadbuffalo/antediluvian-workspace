# Foundational annotated catalogue

**Checked:** 2026-11-10. **Not setting canon.** IDs are permanent. VERIFIED means only that the specified passage or bibliographic fact was checked; PARTIAL means that part of the record, commonly the manuscript apparatus, remains unchecked. A modern website's crawl date is never a composition date.

Records follow SOURCE-ENTRY-TEMPLATE fields. Bibliographic metadata, textual access and physical-witness verification have separate statuses. No translation, catalogue or object establishes independently that a supernatural event occurred. The abbreviated provenance profiles below are inherited explicitly by claims citing them.

## SRC-001 — Genesis, Hebrew text and JPS 1917

- **Status/category/context:** VERIFIED selected passage readings; PARTIAL transmission dossier. Tier I, Jewish Torah and Christian Old Testament. Genesis is canonical in those traditions; this does not make every interpretation shared.
- **Attribution/composition:** Traditional Mosaic attribution belongs to reception. The text does not supply a modern composition date. NABRE's introduction proposes sixth/fifth-century BCE editing; Carr offers a stratified formation model (SRC-004/008). Those are scholarly models, not dates of Adam or Cain. Earlier materials and exact redaction dates remain disputed. No particular documentary hypothesis is adopted by the setting.
- **Witnesses/languages:** Hebrew. Ancient Genesis fragments survive among the Dead Sea Scrolls; exact coverage/dating of relevant fragments has NOT been collated here. The complete medieval Masoretic comparison anchor is Leningrad, NLR Evr. I B 19 A, dated by the holding institution 1008–1010 CE (SRC-006). The online Hebrew text is an edited Masoretic presentation, not an ancient manuscript facsimile or a diplomatic transcription of Leningrad.
- **Edition/access:** *The Holy Scriptures According to the Masoretic Text: A New Translation* (Jewish Publication Society, 1917), English, paired online with Mechon-Mamre's Hebrew text. [Edition description](https://www.mechon-mamre.org/p/pt/pt0.htm); [Genesis 4](https://www.mechon-mamre.org/p/pt/pt0104.htm); [Genesis 5](https://www.mechon-mamre.org/p/pt/pt0105.htm). Replace final chapter digits `04` with `01`–`11` for other chapters. English-only pages use `/e/et/et0101.htm`, etc.; some routes failed, so Hebrew/English and NABRE were also used.
- **Loci checked:** Genesis 1–11, with close attention to 1:26–30; 2:10–24; 3:7,16–24; all of 4–5; 6:1–22; 7:6; 8:20–22; 9:1–7,18–29; 10:1–12,32; 11:1–32. Claim details: CLAIM-001–030 and selected comparisons in 031–034,043–044.
- **Difficulties:** Compressed genealogies; no ages for Cain's descendants; recurring but distinct names; 4:7 syntax; 4:8 speech; 4:21 instruments; 4:22 craft description and material terms; 4:26 invocation; differing chapter-5 figures across textual traditions. An English heading such as “Beginning of Civilization” is editorial, not an additional Hebrew verse.
- **Rights:** JPS 1917 underlying translation is public domain in the US. Do not assume that fact licenses modern site design, photographs or revised translations. This project stores paraphrase and pointers, not a copied Bible.
- **Reception/limits:** Genesis's named crafts are textual testimony, not a comprehensive industrial census or an archaeological sequence. Named sons are not a population total. Translation comparison cannot decide which ancient reading is earliest.
- **Setting uses:** Source floor for agriculture, households, city naming, portable pastoral life, music, metalwork, moral agency and violence. All added institutions remain IDEA.
- **Verification log:** Online passages checked 2026-11-10; no physical manuscript inspected. Further work: critical Hebrew apparatus and exact Qumran coverage; Samaritan comparison via a reliable edition.

## SRC-002 — Greek Genesis in NETS

- **Status/category/context:** VERIFIED selected translation readings and translator's stated base; PARTIAL Greek apparatus/witness audit. Tier I textual tradition: ancient Jewish Greek translation, subsequently received by Christians.
- **Composition/witness distinction:** Pentateuch translation generally placed in third-century BCE Egypt; [Oxford scholarly overview](https://academic.oup.com/reference/62341/reference-article-abstract/561720572). This dates a translation project, not the surviving PDF or a particular manuscript. An early relevant witness is **P.Fouad 266a / Rahlfs 942 / LDAB 3450**, dated first century BCE in Hurtado's scholarly catalogue, with parts of Genesis 4:5–7,23 among its listed remains (SRC-019). This does not establish every word or numeral in our selected text. Leaf-by-leaf collation remains open; do not cite “the fourth-century Bible” indiscriminately for Genesis 1–11. The official Sinaiticus project says most of its historical books are now missing (SRC-006).
- **Edition:** Robert J. V. Hiebert, “Genesis,” in Albert Pietersma and Benjamin G. Wright, eds., *A New English Translation of the Septuagint* (Oxford University Press, 2007); [electronic edition](https://ccat.sas.upenn.edu/nets/edition/), second-printing masters (2009), with corrections through 2014. [Genesis PDF](https://ccat.sas.upenn.edu/nets/edition/01-gen-nets.pdf).
- **Base:** Hiebert's “To the Reader,” opening paragraphs: Wevers 1974, incorporating identified changes from Wevers's 1993 *Notes*. Consequently “NETS” is not a physical ancient witness and is not identical to every Greek manuscript.
- **Loci:** Genesis 4:7–8,17–26; 5:3–31; 6:1–4; 7:6. Printed pp. 8–10 are the useful comparison area. CLAIM-030–034,044.
- **Readings/limits:** Names and numbers differ from the Hebrew baseline. Keep the selected NETS figures rather than inventing one uniform “Septuagint chronology.” Full apparatus was not read. Compare kin relationships before equating spelling variants.
- **Rights:** Modern copyrighted translation; no extended quotations retained. Use independent concise paraphrase and bibliographic references.
- **Setting uses:** Demonstrates genuine ancient interpretive/textual diversity; supplies optional chronological models, not automatic fictional truth.
- **Verification log:** PDF's searchable text and introduction read 2026-11-10. Direct PDF download and screenshot endpoints intermittently failed; no claim of visual manuscript collation or complete apparatus verification. Numerals were checked in the extracted passage text, then arithmetic checked independently.

## SRC-009 — Josephus, Jewish Antiquities I

- **Status/category/context:** VERIFIED selected Whiston translation passages; PARTIAL Greek edition/manuscript audit. Tier IV ancient Jewish reception of Genesis in a Greco-Roman historiographical work.
- **Authorship/date:** Flavius Josephus; composed c. 93/94 CE. *Antiquities* 20.267 dates its completion to Domitian's thirteenth year: [primary statement](https://lexundria.com/j_aj/20.267/wst). This is many centuries later than proposed first-millennium BCE Genesis composition layers and is not testimony from the narrated primeval age.
- **Witnesses/languages:** Original Greek, transmitted in medieval manuscript traditions. Earliest extant witness for these exact sections not established in this pass; a critical Greek edition and stemma remain needed. Do not confuse Whiston's publication with an ancient witness.
- **Edition/access:** William Whiston translation (first published 1737), accessible at [Chicago/LacusCurtius, Book I](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Josephus/ant-1.html) and [Lexundria with section numbers](https://lexundria.com/j_aj/1/wst). Public-domain translation; current digital transcription not collated against a specified 1737 copy. Cite section numbers, not website paragraph counting alone.
- **Loci:** 1.60–62 (Cain, wealth, measures, boundaries, walls, compelled family); 1.63–66 (crafts and growing violence); 1.68–71 (Seth's descendants, astronomy, pillars). CLAIM-035–038. Parallel older chapter notation: 1.2.2–3.
- **Disagreements/limits:** Material added beyond Genesis. Josephus's moralized picture of measures and settlement need not be the world's objective truth. The old English wording of 1.60 can mislead about Nod as a city; keep 1.62's explicit Enoch naming and consult Greek before resolving that wording. Claim that a pillar survived in Siriad is a literary assertion, not an authenticated archaeological find.
- **Setting use/log:** Ancient precedent for coercive urbanism, economic control and preservation of knowledge. None automatically adopted. Passages and completion statement checked 2026-11-10; no “earliest invention of this motif” claim made.
